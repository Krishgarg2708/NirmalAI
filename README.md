# 🌱 NirmalAI

### AI-Powered Urban Waste Intelligence & Response Platform

> **Predict. Prioritize. Clean. Verify.**

NirmalAI is an AI-first civic technology platform designed to transform urban waste management from a **reactive complaint-based system into a predictive, intelligent, and verifiable workflow**.

Instead of simply reporting garbage, NirmalAI helps authorities:

**Detect → Analyze → Predict → Prioritize → Optimize → Clean → Verify**

The platform combines **Computer Vision, Predictive Analytics, Geospatial Intelligence, AI-based Prioritization, Route Optimization, and Cleanup Verification** into one unified municipal command center.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

India generates enormous amounts of municipal waste every day, but waste management remains largely reactive.

A typical workflow looks like:

```text
Garbage appears
      ↓
Citizen notices it
      ↓
Complaint is submitted
      ↓
Authority receives complaint
      ↓
Collection team is dispatched
      ↓
Garbage is removed
      ↓
Incident marked "Resolved"
